Text messages

We'll text you when your order is ready

That's the only kind of text TJ's Kitchen sends. No ads, no specials, no mailing list by text — just a heads-up about the order you placed.

How it works

  • When you order online from one of our events, checkout shows an optional box: “Text me when it's ready.” It is never ticked for you — you tick it yourself and type your mobile number.
  • We text you about that order only: when it's ready (or on its way). Usually 1–2 messages per order.
  • Ordering never requires texts. Leave the box empty and we'll call your name at the trailer as usual.
  • Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts, HELP for help.
  • We never sell or share your number. Privacy & texting terms
