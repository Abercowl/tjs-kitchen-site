Hello friends, neighbors, and family!

John and I are so happy to announce that we finally have the kitchen trailer we've been working toward. We're now up and running five days a week, and we're available to host tournaments as well as corporate and community events.

As promised, to thank you for your support over the years, we're hosting a Free Hot Dog Day at Camarillo Springs! We'd love for you to come by and let us show our appreciation. We've cooked through rain, high winds, and even a hurricane, and we've missed graduations and holidays along the way. Your support is what kept us going. Sign up below and we'll send you all the event details.

Sadly, one of our earliest supporters passed away before he could see what his kindness helped build. No matter who he teed off with, by the turn they were best friends. He was always a joy to see. His passing taught us to cherish the time we have with friends, family, and community. We always think there's more time, but there isn't always.

Mike Brakebill, we miss you so very much. We wish we'd spent more time getting to know you.

With love and gratitude,
John & Tyanna
