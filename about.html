The kitchen at Camarillo Springs

Who we are

TJ's Kitchen is the food stop at Camarillo Springs Golf Course. Whether you're grabbing breakfast before your tee time, a sandwich at the turn, or something for the group after your round, we've got you covered.

We're a local, independently run kitchen, and we built our own ordering system so you can order from your phone and get your food without waiting in line. You can even have it brought out to you on the course.
