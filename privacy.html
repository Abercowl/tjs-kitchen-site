Privacy Policy

How TJ's Kitchen collects, uses and protects your information.

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy covers TJ's Kitchen (“we”, “us”), the food trailer at Camarillo Springs Golf Course, 791 Camarillo Springs Rd, Camarillo, CA. It applies to tjskitchenvc.com, our online ordering site at order.tjskitchenvc.com, our iPhone app, and our order-ready text messages. Our Terms & Conditions, including our text message terms, are part of this policy.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to take and fill your order.
  • Your mobile number is used for one thing only: texting you when your order is ready (or on its way), and only if you ask us to.
  • Email is used for everything else: receipts, replies to your questions, and our email list if you join it.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • Square processes all card payments. We never see or store your full card number.

Information we collect

  • Order details: what you ordered, when, and how you want to receive it (pick-up at the trailer or delivery to your hole or seat).
  • Your name, so we can match the order to you.
  • Your email address, if you give it to us for a receipt, an account, a question or our email list.
  • Your mobile number, only if you tick the optional “Text me when it's ready” box at checkout.
  • Payment information is entered directly into Square's secure payment form. We receive only a confirmation and basic details such as the card brand and last four digits.
  • iPhone app: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can tell you when your order is ready.
  • On your own device: our ordering site may remember your cart and the mobile number you entered in your browser's storage, so you don't have to type them again. You can clear this by clearing your browser data.

How we use it

  • To make, deliver and hand over your order, and to take payment for it.
  • Mobile numbers: only to send order status texts about the order you placed. We never use your mobile number for marketing, promotions, surveys or anything else, and we never add it to any list.
  • Email: to send receipts and order updates, answer your questions, and send our email newsletter about events and menu news if you sign up for it. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep basic business records (sales and tax records) as the law requires.

How we share it

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. This exclusion applies to every category of sharing listed below.

We use a small number of service providers who handle information only to run our service for us, under their own privacy and security obligations:

  • Square — processes card payments and records sales.
  • Twilio — delivers our order-ready text messages to your phone.
  • Supabase and Vercel — host our ordering system and website.
  • Our email provider — sends receipts and newsletters.

We may also disclose information if required by law, such as a valid court order.

Text messages

Order-ready texts are sent only if you opt in, are about your order only, and contain no marketing. Message frequency: usually 1–2 messages per order. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to texts is never a condition of purchase. Full details are in our text message terms.

How long we keep it

We keep order and sales records for as long as we need them for taxes and accounting. Mobile numbers given for order-ready texts are kept with that order record and are not reused for future orders unless you opt in again. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below), except where the law requires us to keep it.

Security

Our systems use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access is limited to TJ's Kitchen staff who need it, and administrator accounts require two-step sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text, or simply leave the box unticked.
  • Email list: use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter.
  • See, correct or delete your information: call or email us using the details below. California residents may have additional rights under California law, and we will honor them.

Children

Our online services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
