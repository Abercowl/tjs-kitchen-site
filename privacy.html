Privacy & Texting Terms

How TJ's Kitchen handles your information, and how our order-update text messages work.

Last updated November 9, 2026

Who we are

TJ's Kitchen is the food trailer at Camarillo Springs Golf Course, 791 Camarillo Springs Rd, Camarillo, CA. Questions about this page: call (805) 670-5686 or email tjskitchenvc0001@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your order: what you ordered, when, the total and tip, and how you want it (pickup, the hole and cart number for course delivery, or The Local).
  • Your name, so we can call out your order.
  • Your mobile number — only if you tick “Text me when it's ready” at checkout.
  • Your email — only if you join our mailing list.
  • Payment: card and Apple Pay / Google Pay payments are handled by Square. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

To make and hand over your order, to send the order updates you asked for, to answer questions and handle refunds, and to keep our own sales records. If you joined the mailing list, to send our specials and where we're cooking next — you can unsubscribe any time from any email.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use a few service providers only to run our ordering: Square (payments), Twilio (sending text messages), Supabase (our order database) and Vercel (hosting this website). They may use your information only to provide those services to us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.

Text message terms (“TJ's Kitchen Order Updates”)

  • How you sign up: tick “Text me when it's ready” and enter your mobile number when you check out at order.tjskitchenvc.com. Signing up is optional and is not required to order.
  • What we send: updates about that order only — when it's ready for pickup or on its way to you. No marketing texts.
  • How often: usually 1, and at most 2 messages per order.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • Stop: reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. You'll get one confirmation and nothing after that. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Help: reply HELP, or call (805) 670-5686.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept as long as we need them for our business and tax records. You can ask us to remove your mobile number or email at any time.

Your choices

You can order without giving a phone number or email, reply STOP to any text, unsubscribe from any email, or contact us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you. California residents have these rights under California law.

Children

Our ordering site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this page, we'll update the date at the top.
