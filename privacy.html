Privacy & Texting Terms

How we handle your information and our text messages.

Last updated November 9, 2026

The short version

  • We only keep what we need to serve you: your name, your order, and your mobile number or email if you give them to us for texts, order updates or our email list.
  • We don't sell or share your information.
  • Square handles all payments. We never see or store your card number.
  • Want your information removed? Call (805) 670-5686 or email tjskitchen0001@gmail.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.

Text messages

Texts are optional and never required to buy anything.

  • Specials: sign up on our website or sign-up sheet, then reply JOIN to confirm (or just text JOIN to our number). Up to 4 messages a month.
  • Order updates: tick “Text me when it's ready” when ordering at an event. 1–2 messages per order.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to stop all texts, HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
